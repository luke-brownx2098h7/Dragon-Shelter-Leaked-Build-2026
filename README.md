## 🕹️ About

Dragon Shelter is a cozy fantasy farming and life sim about rebuilding your grandfather’s abandoned farm and restoring the bond between humans and dragons. Grow crops, raise unique dragon companions, explore magical biomes, cook, craft, and help a forgotten community recover. This is the raw pre-release build that slipped out before the real thing was ready.

For players who want a gentle single-player farm sim with dragons, exploration, crafting, and a rough-edged early-build atmosphere.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🐉 **Dragon Companions** — Befriend dragons with unique traits, powers, and personalities.
- 🌱 **Farm Restoration** — Rebuild an abandoned farm and turn it into a living dragon sanctuary.
- 🗺️ **Fantasy Exploration** — Explore colorful magical biomes and uncover the world’s hidden secrets.
- 🍳 **Cooking** — Gather ingredients, discover recipes, and prepare enchanted dishes.
- 🔨 **Crafting & Upgrades** — Gather resources, upgrade tools, restore buildings, and create new stations.
- 🏡 **Community Stories** — Build relationships with townsfolk and help bring their struggling town back to life.
- ✨ **Dragon Abilities** — Use each dragon’s elemental abilities to support farming and exploration.
- 🎨 **Hand-Painted World** — Wander through a warm, painterly fantasy world built for slow discovery.
